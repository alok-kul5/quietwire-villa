# 09. Resident Quick Guide

*Keep this on the fridge or in the utility room. Two pages, plain language.*

## Your house in one minute

Your home is wired, not wireless. Light switches, sensors, cameras and Wi-Fi points are connected by cable. If the internet goes down, **lights, fans, air conditioning, ventilation, locks and the water leak protection keep working.**

## Light

- **Wall buttons always work.** Press for the normal scene, press again for off.
- Lights get **warmer and dimmer in the evening** on purpose. This is meant to help you wind down.
- **Night trips:** walking to the bathroom or kitchen at night turns on a very dim, warm light. It never switches to bright.
- Want full brightness for a task? Press and hold the button. It returns to normal after a while.

## Air

- A small sensor in each main room checks CO2, dust (PM2.5) and chemical smells (VOC).
- The ventilation speeds up by itself when air gets stale. A little fan sound is normal. At night it runs quietly.
- **If a notice says "poor air":** open nothing at first. Check the cause (cooking, incense, cleaning products, new paint or furniture), switch on the kitchen extract if cooking, and give it 15 minutes. If it stays high or you smell gas, see "Emergencies" below.
- **Outdoor air is poor** (smog, dust, smoke): the system reduces fresh-air intake. Keep windows shut on those days.
- Windows: opening one pauses the air conditioning in that room.

## Water

- The **main water valve** closes by itself if a leak sensor gets wet or water keeps running. You get a notice on your phone and the wall keypad light.
- **To reopen:** find the leak first, wipe the sensor, then press **Water: Open** on the keypad or app.
- Drinking water quality (TDS) is tracked. If you see "Check filter", call the number on the service sticker. Do not ignore it.

## Wi-Fi and wireless

- Bedrooms have **no Wi-Fi point inside**. At night (default 11 pm to 6 am) the Wi-Fi covering the bedrooms switches off. Wired sockets in every bedroom keep working.
- To use Wi-Fi at night: press **Wi-Fi Night Off** on the lounge keypad to turn it on for 2 hours.
- You can still use your phone as normal. This design only removes always-on fixed sources near where you sleep.

## Security and doors

- **Modes:** Home (cameras on outside only), Away (everything armed), Night (doors and windows armed, motion inside ignored).
- Main door opens with the app, the keypad code or the key. Keep the mechanical key somewhere safe.
- No cameras in bedrooms or bathrooms. Any indoor camera is your choice and stays off when you are home.

## Energy

The dashboard shows electricity use by area and solar generation. Run the water heater and charge the car when the sun is up to save money.

## Everyday scenes (examples)

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Good morning | Opens curtains and warms up the light, kitchen lights on |
| Evening | Warm dim light in living, dining and lounge |
| Movie | Dim lights, curtains closed |
| Good night | Lights off, doors checked, night mode on, quiet ventilation |
| Leaving | Lights off, away mode on, valve flow limit tightened |

## Emergencies

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Fire | Leave the house. Call the fire service. The fire alarm is separate from the smart system and cannot be silenced by it |
| Gas smell | Do not touch switches. Open windows if safe, leave, and call the gas provider |
| Water flooding | Close the main valve manually (the lever is in the utility room) if it has not already closed, and switch off the geyser |
| Power cut | The rack has a UPS. Lights and sockets on the backup circuit stay on, and the system alerts you |
| Internet down | Nothing to do. Local functions continue. Remote access and notifications resume when it returns |
| System acting strange | Do **not** reset or power-cycle things at random. Call the support number |

## House rules for guests

- Guest Wi-Fi is separate and has no access to the house systems.
- Don't unplug anything in the utility/rack room.
- Please don't hold the keypad buttons, press once and wait a second.
